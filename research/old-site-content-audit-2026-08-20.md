## 1. Missing entirely — commercial

### 1a. The cancer offer (biggest gap)
The old `/how-we-treat-cancer` page is the site's main commercial page. None of it carried over except a two-line "Cancer Support" card.

Facts on that page and nowhere on the new site:
- The **Herbernie Cancer/HIV Kit** as a named, purchasable product
- **R45 900** for the kit · **+R800** overnight delivery within SA · international quoted on destination
- **~5-month** full protocol, with measurable progress typically observed in that window
- Kit is a **foundational base customised with additional medicines per cancer type**
- Includes a **compact frequency generator**
- **24/7 direct access to Dr. Lurie** for emergencies and treatment queries
- The "**elimination crisis**" / retracing concept — symptoms may temporarily worsen as cancer cells die
- Explicit "we cannot offer guarantees" statement

⚠️ **Decision needed, not just a copy-paste.** The old page opens with a direct curative claim. The new site was deliberately written in supportive/adjunctive language with a medical disclaimer. Migrating this page verbatim would reverse that positioning and carry regulatory risk. Recommend: keep the product, kit structure, timeline, support model and price; rewrite the claims.

- Remote consultation (WhatsApp / Zoom) — **R490**

### 1d. "Coming soon" offerings from the old home page

## 2. Missing entirely — practical / policy

### 2a. FAQ page (7 Q&As, none carried over)
- **Not covered by medical aid** — private practice, including third-party testing (e.g. Ampath) requested by Dr. Lurie
- Reports — EIS scan emailed free; full written report R450, 3–4 working days; patients encouraged to take their own notes/photos/video **for personal use only, not public sharing**

- Tinctures/supplements — use **at least 3 months** or until follow-up; follow-up suggested **after a month**; if treatment is advised, **minimum 10 sessions**
- Ordering — send list → quotation + banking details; **quote valid 3 working days**; proof of payment via WhatsApp or email; **nothing ships until payment reflects**; full address + postal code required

### 2b. Legal pages — all four missing
Disclaimer (9 sections), Terms and Conditions (10 sections), Quality Control Policy (7 sections), Privacy Policy. The new site carries a single footer sentence.

For a practice selling ingestible herbal products this is the most exposed gap after the claims language. The Disclaimer in particular contains the "no medical claims / testimonials are not a guarantee of results" wording that the Patient Accounts section arguably relies on.

### 2c. Rife contraindications — **safety omission**
The old Rife page names who must not be treated: **pregnant people, people with epilepsy, people at risk of heart failure, people wearing a pacemaker or life-support device, and people in acute/critical/life-support care.** The new site offers Rife Sessions with no such warning.

Note the new Patient Accounts section includes an **epilepsy** testimonial while the old site lists epilepsy as a Rife contraindication. Worth raising with the client — it reads as a contradiction.

The old page also carries a graphene / COVID-vaccination note. Recommend leaving that one out.

## 3. Compressed — detail lost

Six full treatment pages became six one-line cards. Specifics that disappeared:

**EIS Scan** — that it is non-invasive and used for pre-screening, treatment planning and monitoring progress; measures electrolyte/enzyme activity; **37 internal organs across 69 parameters**; acid-base status; biochemical, hormonal and neurohormonal estimates; mitochondrial and enzymatic activity; interstitial pH/O₂/CO₂/ATP; BMI and body composition; organ system and health risk analysis. Positioned as faster and more sensitive than traditional blood tests. There is also a **video** on that page.

**Rife** — the resonance-frequency mechanism, ~18 claimed benefits, the conditions-of-use disclaimer, and the contraindications above.

**Mineralizing Foot Detox** — the mechanism (electrode in a container of water, feet submerged, **~45 minutes**, minerals created by the electrode reacting with the water) and ~18 claimed benefits.

**Spinal Alignment Bed** — jade rollers head-to-base-of-spine, infra-red penetration, 7 benefits, **plus its own 6-question FAQ**: minimum age 13 / no maximum, **40-minute** sessions, treatments done fully clothed, far-infrared vs UV safety, hydrate afterwards, consult a GP first for serious back conditions.

**G5 Massage** — deep-tissue vibration mechanism, 5 benefits, the practitioner's first-person account of why they use it, **the attachment heads and what each is for** (flat = smoothing/toning, knobbed = circulation/cellulite, roller/brush = drainage), and what to expect (**1–2× per week**, possible temporary redness or mild discomfort, hydrate before and after).

**Neural Therapy** — that it targets the **autonomic nervous system**, and that it works by **injecting local anaesthetic into defined regions of the body**. The new card says "injection-based therapy" without saying what is injected or why. Five listed benefits also missing.
