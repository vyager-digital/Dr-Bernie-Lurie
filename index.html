In more detail

EIS Scan & Consultation

An Electro Interstitial Scan is non-invasive. It is used for pre-screening, to help shape a treatment plan, and to follow how things are moving over the course of care.

It measures electrolyte and enzyme activity, and reports on the functional status of 37 internal organs across 69 parameters. The output covers acid-base status, biochemical, hormonal and neurohormonal estimates, cellular mitochondrial and enzymatic activity, interstitial pH, oxygen, CO2 and ATP, body mass index and body composition, and an organ system and health risk analysis.

The scan is one input among several. Bernie reads it alongside your history, your symptoms and the rest of the assessment. It is not a diagnosis on its own, and it does not replace tests your doctor has ordered.

Rife Sessions

The Rife-Medic is a non-invasive device that applies specific frequencies through hand-held contact cylinders. The idea behind it is resonance: living organisms each have their own characteristic frequency, and the device applies frequencies intended to disturb microbes while leaving surrounding tissue undisturbed.

In the practice it is used to support circulation, detoxification, energy and pain relief, as part of a wider plan rather than on its own.

Conditions of use. The device is not intended to diagnose disease or to replace any medication or therapy your doctor has prescribed. Use of any bio-resonance device is at your own discretion and risk.

Not suitable for everyone. Rife sessions are not offered to anyone who is pregnant, has epilepsy, is at risk of heart failure, wears a pacemaker or any other life-support device, or is receiving acute or critical care.

Mineralising Foot Detox

The treatment is simple and quite relaxing. An electrode sits in a container of water, you submerge your feet, and you rest for roughly 45 minutes. Minerals are produced by the reaction between the electrode and the water.

Patients commonly report better sleep, improved digestion, more energy, relief from stress and tension, and reduced inflammation. Effects vary from person to person, and it works best as part of a wider plan rather than as a one-off.

Spinal Alignment Bed

A thermal massage bed combining gentle spinal decompression, acupressure and heat. Physical jade rollers work from the head to the base of the spine, and the bed produces far-infrared warmth that penetrates into the tissue.

It is used to encourage circulation, ease muscular tension, relieve stress and fatigue, support detoxification and settle everyday aches.

Sessions run 40 minutes and are done fully clothed. The minimum age is 13, and there is no upper limit. Far-infrared is a different thing from ultraviolet and does not carry the same risks. Drink plenty of water afterwards. If you have a serious back condition, speak to your GP before booking.

G5 Massage

The G5 works by vibration. Shaped probes deliver vibration energy into the deeper layers of tissue, which helps relieve pain, move circulation along and relax muscle. It reaches areas a manual massage struggles with, and it holds the same rhythm throughout.

The heads each do something different: flat heads for smoothing and toning, knobbed heads to stimulate circulation and work on cellulite, and roller or brush heads for gentle drainage. The machine moves up, down and side to side, creating a percussion effect that mobilises fluid and brings oxygen into the tissue.

What to expect. A course of 1–2 sessions per week is usually suggested. Temporary redness or mild discomfort afterwards is normal given the depth of the massage. Drink plenty of water before and after.

Neural Therapy

Neural therapy targets the autonomic nervous system, the part of the nervous system that runs the body's automatic functions: immune response, circulation and hormone production.

It works by injecting a local anaesthetic into defined regions of the body, with the intention of resetting nerve pathways that have stopped regulating properly.

It is used mainly for chronic musculoskeletal pain, headaches and migraine, and for autonomic complaints such as digestive trouble and menstrual irregularity. It may also support circulation and tissue repair, and calm inflammation.

Suitability and risks are discussed with you at consultation before anything is agreed.

Who Rife sessions are not for

Rife sessions are not suitable for everyone. They are not offered to anyone who is pregnant, has epilepsy, is at risk of heart failure, wears a pacemaker or any other life-support device, or is receiving acute or critical care.

If any of these apply to you, tell Bernie before you book. Other options are available, and your consultation is unaffected.

The Herbernie Support Kit

A structured programme of roughly five months, built around a foundational kit and adapted to your situation. It is designed to sit alongside the care of your oncology or medical team.

The kit came out of many years of work in the practice. Rather than hand patients a list of separate products, Dr. Lurie set out to bring the elements he drew on most often into one considered programme. It has been revised many times since, as new understanding and new components were added.

Every person responds differently. Metabolism, physical condition, immune strength, emotional state and outlook all affect how the body responds to any supportive programme. That is why the foundational kit is adapted for each patient rather than issued the same way every time.

What the programme involves

The kit forms the base. Additional preparations are then selected for your particular situation, and dietary and nutritional guidance is built around them. Progress is reviewed with you as you go, and the programme is adjusted where it needs to be.

Some patients notice symptoms they had felt before returning for a period during the programme. In naturopathic practice this is described as retracing. If it happens, tell Dr. Lurie. He will talk it through with you, and where it is appropriate, alongside your treating doctor.

Support while you are on it

Five months is a long time to carry a serious diagnosis, and questions rarely arrive during office hours. You have Dr. Lurie's direct number for the duration of the programme, for emergencies and for treatment queries.

Questions

Before you get in touch

The questions the practice is asked most often. If yours is not here, send it through and we will answer it directly.

Are you registered with medical aids?

We are a private practice and are not covered by medical aid. That includes any third-party testing, such as Ampath, requested by Dr. Lurie.

Can I get a report after my consultation?

A copy of your EIS scan is emailed to you at no charge. A full written report from Dr. Lurie is available at an additional cost of R450 and takes 3–4 working days.

You are welcome to take notes, photographs or video during your session. Please keep these for your own personal use rather than sharing them publicly.

Am I allowed to eat or drink before the consultation?

Yes. You may eat and drink as normal before your consultation.

How long should I use the tinctures and supplements?

Tinctures and supplements should be used for at least 3 months, or until your next follow-up consultation. A follow-up is usually suggested after a month.

Where Dr. Lurie advises in-practice treatment, we suggest a minimum of 10 sessions.

Can I order more tinctures or supplements, and do you courier?

Yes. We use The Courier Guy for local deliveries. A flat R120 is added to each order to cover the courier cost, with a lead time of 2–3 working days.

How do I place an order?
  1. Send us a list of the tinctures or supplements you need to replace. We will reply with a quotation and banking details. Quotations are valid for 3 working days.
  2. Send proof of payment by WhatsApp or email.
  3. Include your full street address and postal code so we can send your parcel.

No parcels are dispatched until payment reflects in our bank account.

Is there anyone who should not have a Rife session?

Yes. Rife sessions are not offered to anyone who is pregnant, has epilepsy, is at risk of heart failure, wears a pacemaker or any other life-support device, or is receiving acute or critical care.

Tell Dr. Lurie before you book if any of these apply to you. Other options are available, and your consultation is unaffected.
