03 The one gap

Trust at the point of payment

11 completed —— 0 reached payment

No one is abandoning at checkout, because no one gets that far. The drop is the decision to book, after she reads her results. Three things say it is trust, not price.

Not the price
A gynaecologist visit runs R3,700. The consultation is R490, an eighth of that. The cheapest serious option is the one not converting.
Not the mechanics
Zero payment attempts. PayFast and the booking flow are not where people fall away. They decide not to start.
It’s the leap
Paying upfront, online, to a practitioner she has never spoken to, off one automated email. That is the hardest moment to convert.

One honest caveat: eleven leads is still a small sample. We cannot call the booking step broken on the numbers alone. The pattern is clear enough to act on, and the lead cost is low enough to justify a little more spend to be sure.

The plan

The next move is trust, not budget

1
Rebuild the results screen and follow-up email to build trust
Dr. Lurie’s credentials and photo, a real patient outcome, what the R490 includes, and a softer first step such as a short introductory call before payment.
2
Resume the campaign, let it run to ~R3,000–R5,000
Enough spend for the booking rate to become a number we can trust, not a coin toss.
3
Keep tuning budget across the ads
A secondary efficiency gain while the trust work does the heavy lifting.

The leads are real and cheap. The next move is trust.
