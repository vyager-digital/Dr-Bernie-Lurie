02 The page & quiz

Everyone who starts, finishes

All 11 women who began the quiz completed it, in two to five minutes each. A healthy quiz funnel runs near 40%. This one converts the women who engage at close to 100%.

03 The one gap

Trust at the point of payment

11 completed —— 0 reached payment

No one is abandoning at checkout, because no one gets that far. The drop is the decision to book, after she reads her results. Three things say it is trust, not price.

Not the price
The R490 is on screen from the first step and stays there through the whole quiz. Everyone who saw it still finished. They are not stopping at the number.
Not the mechanics
Zero payment attempts. PayFast and the booking flow are not where people fall away. They decide not to start.
A trust barrier
Paying upfront, online, to a practitioner she has never spoken to, off one automated email. Research shows this is the hardest moment to convert.

One honest caveat: eleven leads is still a small sample. We cannot call the booking step broken on the numbers alone. The pattern is clear enough to act on, and the lead cost is low enough to justify a little more spend to be sure.

The fix

What she’d see instead

Same result, same price. The screen simply does more of the reassuring a person would do if she could speak to someone before paying.

The plan

The next move is trust, not budget

At R490 a consultation, the campaign covers its own ad cost once roughly one lead in five books. The leads are already priced to make that work. The constraint is the payment step, not the budget.

1
Rebuild the results screen as a trust handoff, not a checkout
Her result is the moment she feels understood. Right now the next thing she sees is a price and a pay button. We turn it into a screen that earns the booking: a patient story like hers beside the button, an honest fair-and-fit reassurance, and a quiet way to ask a question first. The follow-up email carries the same.
2
Resume the campaign, let it run to ~R3,000–R5,000
Enough spend for the booking rate to become a number we can trust, not a coin toss.
3
Keep tuning budget across the ads
A secondary efficiency gain while the trust work does the heavy lifting.

The economics work. What’s left is earning the booking.
