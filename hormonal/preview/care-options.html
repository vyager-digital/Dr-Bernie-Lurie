The first step

A simple care path for patients.

Patients do not need to choose a long programme before they have been assessed. The first step is the EIS Scan and Consultation. After that, Dr. Lurie explains the recommended treatment path.

1

Scan

The EIS scan gives a full-body overview and helps identify areas of imbalance in the body.

2

Consultation

Dr. Lurie reviews your scan, symptoms and health history with you during your appointment.

3

Treatment Plan

You receive a recommended natural treatment plan. Medicines, tinctures or supplements are quoted separately.

Treatment plans

Your treatment plan is based on your assessment.

After the EIS Scan and Consultation, Dr. Lurie recommends the treatment plan that best fits your body and your symptoms. Most plans fall into one of these ranges.

Foundational Plan

For patients who need a lighter treatment plan or support for a narrower health concern.

Comprehensive Plan

R3,450

For patients who need broader support across more than one area of the body.

Intensive Plan

R4,950

Each treatment plan price includes your EIS Scan and Consultation. The R990 you pay to begin comes off the total, so there is nothing added on top.
