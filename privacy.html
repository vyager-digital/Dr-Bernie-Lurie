1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Herbernie International (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website or use our services.

Herbernie International is a natural health practice based in Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth), South Africa, led by Dr. Bernard Lurie. We are the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

By using this website or booking a consultation, you confirm that you have read and understood this policy.

2. What Information We Collect

Contact information

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • WhatsApp or telephone number (where provided)
  • Delivery address and postal code, where you order products

Health information

  • Health history and symptoms shared during consultations
  • Assessment and scan findings, including EIS scan results
  • Consultation notes and recommended treatment plans

Payment information

Payment for products is made by electronic transfer to our bank account. Where online payment is offered, it is processed by a third-party payment provider. We do not collect or store your card details.

Usage information

When you visit our website, we may collect standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This is used only for website performance and analytics purposes.

3. How We Collect It

We collect your personal information in the following ways:

  • Directly from you when you complete the enquiry form, book a consultation, order products, or contact us
  • During your consultation, from information shared verbally or in writing with our practitioners
  • Automatically, as basic usage data collected when you browse the website

We do not purchase, rent, or obtain personal information from third-party data brokers.

4. Why We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and arrange your appointment
  • Carry out your consultation, assessment, and any treatment you agree to
  • Prepare and send reports, scan results, and treatment recommendations
  • Process and deliver product orders
  • Keep the health records we are required to keep by law
  • Maintain and improve the website

We will not use your information for any purpose beyond what is described here without your consent.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Under POPIA, we process your personal information on the following grounds:

  • Contract to fulfil your booking or order and deliver the services you have requested
  • Consent for optional communications, which you can withdraw at any time
  • Legal obligation to retain health records as required by South African law
  • Legitimate interest for website analytics and service improvement, where this does not override your rights

Because we process special personal information (health data), we do so only with your explicit consent, or where it is necessary to carry out your treatment.

6. Who We Share It With

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Our practitioners and clinical staff involved in your care, on a need-to-know basis
  • Third-party testing laboratories where Dr. Lurie requests testing, and only with your agreement
  • Courier services, which receive your delivery address in order to deliver your order
  • Payment providers, who handle payment details under their own privacy policy
  • Communication tools, such as the email and WhatsApp platforms used to send results and appointment reminders
  • Legal requirements, if we are required to disclose by a court order, regulator, or applicable South African law

Any third party we work with is required to handle your information securely and in accordance with POPIA.

7. How Long We Keep It

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law.

  • Health records are retained for a minimum of 5 years following your last consultation, in line with South African health record requirements
  • Contact and booking information is retained for 3 years after your last interaction, then deleted or anonymised
  • Marketing communications are retained until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent

When information is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymised.

8. How We Protect It

We take the security of your personal information seriously. Our measures include:

  • Secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS) across the website
  • Access to health records restricted to authorised practitioners only
  • Payment processing handled by PCI-compliant third-party providers
  • Regular review of our data handling practices

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. While we take every reasonable precaution, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your information has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

9. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject under POPIA, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to deletion, subject to any legal retention obligations
  • Right to object to certain uses of your information, including direct marketing
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior processing
  • Right to complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe your rights have been violated

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 12. We will respond within a reasonable time and in accordance with POPIA requirements.

Information Regulator contact: inforeg@justice.gov.za

10. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors interact with the site. This data is aggregated and anonymised, and it is not linked to your personal identity.

You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect some functionality of the site.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The current version is always available at this URL, with the revision date noted at the top.

If we make material changes that affect how we use your health information, we will notify you directly by email where we hold your contact details.
